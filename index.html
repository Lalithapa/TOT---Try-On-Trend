How it works

From product page
to “that's the one.”

The fitting room moment, at the exact point shoppers decide. No downloads, no accounts, nothing to learn.

  1. 01

    Tap the button

    Shoppers tap Virtual Try On next to the familiar product details. It opens in a window over the page, without leaving your store.

  2. 02

    See it on, live

    The camera follows them as they turn and move, and the product they picked is on them, in the color they selected. Twenty seconds to explore.

  3. 03

    Keep it, buy it

    They keep a short video to share or save, and Add to cart is one tap away, for the exact variant they tried on.

Two ways to try on

Photo-real when it counts.
Free the rest of the time.

Every plan includes photo-real AI try-ons, and the on-device try-on is always there, unlimited and free, when they run out.

Photo-real AI

Fabric, folds and movement, on them.

A live AI video of the shopper wearing your product, rendered from your product photo as they move. Twenty seconds that feel like a mirror.

  • Live 20-second session, or from an uploaded photo
  • The exact color and variant they selected
  • Included in every plan, then usage-based

A visual aid, not a size or fit prediction.

On-device

Unlimited, private, instant.

Body tracking runs in the shopper's browser and maps your product onto them in real time. The camera never leaves their device.

  • Unlimited on every plan, at no cost
  • Takes over when AI try-ons run out
  • Works on phones, tablets and desktops

Best with clean, front-facing product photos.

FAQ

Good to know.

Something else on your mind? Visit support or email developeragentic@gmail.com.

Do shoppers need to download anything or sign up?

No. Virtual Try On opens right on your product page in the shopper's browser. There's no app to install and no account or email to give.

Which products work best?

Clothing with clean product photos, ideally on a plain background: tops, jackets, dresses, knitwear and trousers. Busy lifestyle photos, flat accessories and jewelry give less convincing results. You choose which products show the button.

Does it tell shoppers their size?

No. TryOnTrend is a visual try-on: it shows how a piece looks on the shopper, not how it fits. Keep your size guide on the page.

Where does the shopper's camera video go?

The camera only starts when the shopper chooses it, after a short notice. The on-device try-on never leaves their device. For the photo-real AI try-on, the camera is streamed to our AI partner for that 20-second session; we don't store the video, and the shopper's recording stays on their own device.

How does billing work?

Plans are billed by Shopify every 30 days, on your Shopify invoice, with no trial on paid plans. Each plan includes a monthly number of photo-real AI try-ons. Beyond that, each extra AI try-on is $0.75, up to a $250 spending limit per 30 days that you approve. The Free plan has no charges at all.

Will it slow down my store?

No. The button script is under 10 KB and loads after the page. The try-on itself only loads when a shopper opens it.

Does it work with my theme?

It works with Online Store 2.0 themes as an app block you place in the theme editor, and with older themes through an app embed that adds the button automatically. No code needed.

How do I see if it's working?

Analytics shows try-ons, add-to-carts, orders and revenue placed within 14 days of a try-on, the funnel from button to order, your most tried-on products and shopper ratings. The Activity tab lists every try-on as it happens.
