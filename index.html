05 — Good to know

A few things, clearly.

Virtual try-on should feel helpful and straightforward. Here’s what shoppers and merchants can expect.

Do shoppers need to install anything?

No. Virtual Try On opens directly from the Shopify product page in the shopper’s browser.

Where does the camera video go?

In on-device mode, the video stays on the shopper’s device. In photo-real AI mode, live video is streamed to Decart for the session. The shopper’s try-on recording remains on their device.

Does it predict the right size or fit?

No. TryOnTrend is a visual try-on aid, not a size or fit guarantee. It works best for apparel with clean product imagery; busy lifestyle photos and accessories can be less convincing.

Can merchants choose which products show it?

Yes. Merchants can show the button on all products or choose products and collections, with exclusions that take priority.
