These terms govern your use of the TryOnTrend Shopify app and website (the “Service”). By installing or using TryOnTrend you agree to them on behalf of your store. If you don't agree, don't install or use the Service. Your use of Shopify is also governed by Shopify's own terms.

The service

TryOnTrend adds a virtual try-on to your Shopify product pages: an on-device try-on that runs in the shopper's browser, and a photo-real AI try-on generated by our AI provider. It also provides settings for where the try-on appears and analytics about its use.

The try-on is a visual aid. It shows how a product may look on a shopper and does not predict size or fit. Results depend on product photos, lighting, the shopper's device and connection, and may not be exact.

Plans, billing and cancellation

  • Plans are billed by Shopify through the Shopify Billing API, every 30 days, on your Shopify invoice. Paid plans have no free trial: the first period is charged when you approve the charge in Shopify.
  • Each plan includes a monthly number of photo-real AI try-ons. On paid plans, additional AI try-ons are charged as usage at the published rate, up to the spending limit you approve in Shopify. The Free plan has no charges and a smaller allowance.
  • You can change or cancel your plan at any time in the app. Cancelling a paid plan is prorated by Shopify. Uninstalling the app cancels the subscription.
  • Prices may change; we'll give notice before a change applies to your subscription, and Shopify will ask you to approve any new charges.
