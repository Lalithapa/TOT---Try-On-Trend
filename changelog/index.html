October 7, 2026

  • Stock rule: the try-on button shows only when a product has more than 1 in stock. Change the number, or turn it off, under Products.
  • Pro plan: the spending limit for extra AI try-ons is now $500 per 30 days.
  • The app is now called TryOnTrend Virtual Try-On in your Shopify admin.

October 6, 2026

  • Full-screen live try-on on every phone, tablet and computer, framed to the shopper's screen.
  • Try-on recordings now match the shape of the shopper's screen.
  • Spending limit alerts in the app at 80% and 100%.
  • The app embed no longer shifts your product page while it loads.

October 4, 2026

  • Free plan with 5 photo-real AI try-ons a month.
  • Products: show try-on on every product or a custom selection, with exclusions.
  • Analytics: revenue after try-on, order rate and a funnel from button to order, plus an Activity tab.
  • A Help page and navigation in the Shopify admin menu.

October 2–3, 2026

  • Photo-real live try-on: a 20-second AI video of the shopper wearing the product.
  • Photo try-on from an uploaded picture.
  • Shoppers can save and share their try-on, and rate it afterwards.
  • A guided setup that detects your theme.
