Features

Your fitting room,
your rules.

Two ways to try on, a setup that takes minutes, and analytics that show what it's worth.

Two ways to try on

Photo-real when it counts.
Free the rest of the time.

Every plan includes photo-real AI try-ons, and the on-device try-on is always there, unlimited and free, when they run out.

Photo-real AI

Fabric, folds and movement, on them.

A live AI video of the shopper wearing your product, rendered from your product photo as they move. Twenty seconds that feel like a mirror.

  • Live 20-second session, or from an uploaded photo
  • The exact color and variant they selected
  • Included in every plan, then usage-based

A visual aid, not a size or fit prediction.

On-device

Unlimited, private, instant.

Body tracking runs in the shopper's browser and maps your product onto them in real time. The camera never leaves their device.

  • Unlimited on every plan, at no cost
  • Takes over when AI try-ons run out
  • Works on phones, tablets and desktops

Best with clean, front-facing product photos.

For shoppers

No app.
No sign-up.

The try-on opens over your product page, in the shopper's browser, and Add to cart is one tap away for the exact variant they tried.

Full screen on every phone

The live try-on fills the screen and frames the shopper to it, on any phone, tablet or computer.

Photo try-on

No camera, or prefer not to? Shoppers upload a photo and see themselves wearing the product.

Keep and share

Shoppers save or share their try-on video, shaped to their screen. It stays on their own device.

For merchants

Set it up once.
Control every detail.

Decide where it shows and how it looks, without touching code.

No-code setup

Add the Virtual Try On block in your theme editor, or turn on the app embed for older themes. A guided setup walks you through it.

Matches your brand

Button text, style, colors, size, corners and position are all theme settings. Or open try-on when shoppers tap the product photo.

You choose the products

Show try-on on every product, or hand-pick products and collections. Exclusions always win.

Stock rule

Show the button only when a product has more than a set number in stock (1 by default), so nobody tries on what they can't buy.

Light on your store

The button script is under 10 KB and loads after the page. The try-on loads only when a shopper opens it.

Spending alerts

See your AI try-on usage in the app, with a warning at 80% of your spending limit.

Analytics

Know what trying on is worth.

Every try-on is connected to what happened next, so you see the revenue it brings, not just the clicks.

Revenue you can see

Orders and revenue placed within 14 days of a try-on, order rate, add-to-cart rate and a funnel from button to order.

Every try-on, live

The Activity tab shows each try-on as it happens and what came next: added to cart, shared, ordered, rated.

Hear from shoppers

After a try-on, shoppers can rate it and leave a comment, so you know which products and modes they love.

Privacy by design

Shoppers stay in control.

A camera is personal. TryOnTrend is built so shoppers know what happens, and so you collect nothing you don't need.

Asked first

The camera only starts when the shopper chooses it, after a short notice explaining what happens.

Nothing stored

We don't store shopper video or photos. Their try-on recording stays on their own device.

Anonymous

Analytics use a random id per browser tab. No names, emails, addresses or faces.

Deleted on uninstall

Uninstall the app and your store's try-on data is deleted. See the privacy policy.

Ready when you are

Let them see
themselves in it.

Every feature is on every plan, Free included.
